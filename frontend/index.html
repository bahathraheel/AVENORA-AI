Trust & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how AVENORA AI protects your guest lists, financial records, and private event workspaces while keeping AI outputs transparent and under your control.

Private Firestore Isolation · Server-Side Gemini Keys

Every workspace action is scoped to your verified Firebase user ID, and no API keys or private credentials are ever exposed in the browser.

Who can view or access my event plans, guest rosters, and budgets?
Only you. Every event, task, budget ledger, guest record, vendor contract, timeline, and Co-Pilot thread is stored under your private user path in Cloud Firestore and protected by strict Firestore Security Rules. Other users cannot list, read, or modify your data.
How are my account sessions and AI requests secured?
Sign-in is handled through Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or Email/Password). Whenever you trigger an AI capability, your browser sends a short-lived Firebase ID token to our Node.js and Express backend, which verifies your identity, applies rate limiting and input sanitization, and calls Google Gemini securely from the server.
What event information is shared with Gemini when I use AI features?
When you run the Event Blueprint generator, Budget Advisor, Smart Checklist, Theme Studio, or AI Co-Pilot, only the active event's planning context (type, date, location, guest count, budget totals, theme, and item summaries) is included in that request so the AI gives tailored guidance without asking you to repeat yourself.
Does AVENORA AI invent vendor contact details or live pricing?
No. AVENORA AI is explicitly instructed never to fabricate real-world vendor phone numbers, availability, or guaranteed quotes. All budget splits and advisor notes are labeled as planning estimates, while outreach templates include clean placeholders so you stay in charge of real vendor inquiries.
Are invitations or vendor messages ever sent automatically?
Never. The AI Invitation Studio and Vendor Inquiry Generator draft ready-to-use messages for WhatsApp and email inside your workspace. You review, customize, and copy them before sending anything to your guests or vendors.
Can I edit AI-generated plans or export my saved data?
Yes. You can edit any section of your 16-section Event Blueprint, regenerate individual sections, print a clean dossier, and export all of your saved events and subcollections as a JSON archive anytime from Account Settings.
